Merrymade

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Merrymade does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no server and no tracking. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Your photos

When you add a photo, you pick it with Apple’s photo picker. Merrymade only sees the photos you choose, uses them to draw your card on your iPhone, and never uploads them anywhere.

AI features

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Merrymade can write card messages, design cards from a description, and turn photos into illustrations. These use Apple’s on-device models (Foundation Models and Image Playground) and are covered by Apple’s privacy policy. Merrymade does not send your photos or words to any other company.

Sharing and saving

Your card leaves your iPhone only when you tap Send and choose where it goes, such as Messages, Instagram, Mail, Photos or a printer. If you save a card to Photos, iOS asks for permission first, and Merrymade only adds to your library. It never reads it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The name you sign your cards with, so you don’t have to type it again.
  • Temporary copies of the cards you export, which iOS clears automatically.

Deleting Merrymade deletes all of it.

What Merrymade never does

  • No analytics, ads or third-party tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is none to sell.
  • No access to your contacts, location or photo library beyond the photos you pick.

Children

Merrymade is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Merrymade ever starts collecting data, you’ll be asked first.
